## Status

Blocked behind TEL-129, TEL-130, and TEL-131. Final acceptance slice for the
Five-Floor MVP Demo.

## Design intent

Prove the actual user-visible milestone in a real Godot session rather than
inferring playability from isolated Core/content/client tests.

## Requirements

- Use the production first-slice content pack.
- Use a documented fixed world seed and explicit demo gameplay configuration.
- Launch through the normal Godot application path.
- Use normal player input only; no developer/debug commands.
- Enter the dungeon on floor 1 and legitimately traverse floors 2, 3, 4, and 5.
- Exercise at least one authored monster encounter through the normal trigger
  path.
- Exercise at least one playable authoritative combat action and continue the
  run.
- Exercise at least one authored dungeon feature through normal interaction.
- Acquire at least one first-slice treasure reward into unsecured expedition
  state.
- Reach the designated floor-5 end-of-demo state.
- Verify the required hidden-information and renderer-authority boundaries remain
  intact.
- Record exact acceptance evidence in this ticket and the MVP gate.

## Non-goals

This ticket does not expand the MVP into TEL-127. Save/load, full controller
parity, complete character creation, comprehensive inventory/equipment, combat
item use, full death/Legacy flow, second expedition, and production art remain
post-MVP unless required to fix an actual blocker in the mandated five-floor
route.

## Invariants

The acceptance run must use the same authoritative simulation and production
content boundaries used by the application. A scripted/headless substitute may
support diagnosis but cannot replace the required real Godot observation.

## Save/version impact

None expected; this is an integration/acceptance ticket. Any implementation
change discovered during acceptance must be returned to its owning TEL slice
rather than silently folded into the verification ticket.

## Verification evidence to record

- exact commit/PR;
- exact world seed;
- exact demo gameplay configuration/setup policy;
- Godot executable/runtime version;
- concise keyboard/input route through floors 1–5;
- encounter/combat observation;
- feature observation;
- treasure/unsecured-progress observation;
- floor-5 completion observation;
- focused automated checks; and
- canonical full repository verification.

## Acceptance criteria

Every required checkbox in `docs/gates/FIVE-FLOOR-MVP-DEMO.md` passes with
recorded evidence. At that point the project may call the five-floor Godot build
an MVP demo and return the implementation queue to TEL-126/TEL-127/TEL-128.
